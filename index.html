An AI studio redefining what's possible.

Alternatefact Studios is a next-generation creative production house that harnesses the power of advanced AI to create breathtaking short films, authentic user-generated content, striking concept art, and high-performing social content.

We believe AI isn't just a tool — it's a new creative medium. Our team of directors, storytellers, and technologists blend cinematic craft with cutting-edge generative models to deliver work that feels both futuristic and deeply human.
